    Install Ivy's Recipes

    To install on iPhone/iPad:

    1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari
    2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
    3. Tap "Add" in the top right

    Once installed, you can share photos directly to Ivy's Recipes from other apps!

    To install on Android:

    1. Tap the menu (three dots) in Chrome
    2. Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"
    3. Tap "Install" to confirm

    Once installed, you can share photos directly to Ivy's Recipes from other apps!

    To install on Desktop:

    1. Look for the install icon in the address bar
    2. Or use the browser menu to find "Install Ivy's Recipes"

    The app appears to be already installed! You can find it on your home screen.
